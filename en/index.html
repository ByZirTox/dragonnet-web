Dragonnet, the survival game with your baby dragon

Dragonnet is a free action game where you protect your nest’s treasure with a little dragon. Each run lasts three minutes, is played with one finger and ends with a boss fight. Play it in your browser, on iPhone, on PC and on Android.

Play now in your browser

No download and no account needed: the game starts right in the page. If you prefer an app, Dragonnet is also on Google Play. Both versions share the same game and the same servers.

How a run works

Your dragon attacks on its own. Your job is to move it: dodge the monsters, collect experience crystals and pick a new power at every level. After three minutes the chapter boss arrives. Win, and you bring coins, gems and sometimes new gear back to the nest.

  1. Keep moving. Monster waves grow every second. Standing still means getting surrounded.
  2. Pick your powers. Six weapons, six passives and their evolutions: no two runs play the same.
  3. Beat the boss. The witch, the dragon hunter and the black dragon each have attacks to learn.

The six weapons of your dragon

Your dragon starts with a weapon that belongs to its species and gains more as it levels up, up to four weapons and four passives at the same time. There are six of them, each with its own way of playing:

  • Fireballs fly toward the nearest enemy and eventually pierce several monsters.
  • Fire Breath is a flame that burns everything on its line and ends up spitting behind you too.
  • Embers circle around you and burn the enemies that come close.
  • Storm strikes an enemy with lightning that then bounces to its neighbours.
  • Roar is a shockwave that pushes back and slows the monsters around you.
  • Razor Scale is a scale that comes back like a boomerang.

Each weapon levels up to 5, and it turns into an evolved version when it meets the passive that matches it. Some dragons come with two weapons, and their bonuses add up: the choice of dragon shapes the first minutes of every run.

Chapters, a journey and a story

Runs are played chapter by chapter. The first five take you from the nest meadow to the dark forest, the misty pass, the knights’ domain and the cursed volcano. They are followed by six painted worlds (the cursed woods, the burning desert, the sea of embers, the dragon graveyard, the sunken kingdom and the islands of the sky), then by chapters that go on without end, each harder than the last. Every chapter ends with a boss, and three bosses take turns: the witch, the dragon hunter and the black dragon.

Behind these fights, Dragonnet tells a story: a black dragon that is not quite the enemy it seems, a dragon keeper’s notebook whose pages open chapter after chapter, and twelve lost baby dragons that you bring back to the nest through portals.

All the game modes

  • Chapters: the main mode, with a boss at the end of every three-minute run.
  • Daily challenge: a special rule each day (a swarm of bats, faster enemies and more), with a reward once a day.
  • The endless night: last as long as you can, with exit portals that bring baby dragons back to the nest.
  • Rush Dragon: a defence and merge game, against a bot or against other players.
  • The World: your dragon’s estate, where you feed, train, fish and plant magic rocks that unlock costumes.
  • Guilds: a protected chat, guild wars and a territory conquest every Friday evening.
  • Events: Halloween, the snow festival and the season pass, each with its own shop and rewards.

What you will find in Dragonnet

  • Twelve legendary dragons

    From the Vouivre to Jörmungand, each dragon comes from a real legend and brings its own starting weapon and bonus.

  • Your dragon’s World

    Between runs, feed, train and care for your dragon, go fishing and grow magic rocks in your garden.

  • Rush Dragon

    A second game of duels: summon and merge your dragons, then face other players in arenas.

  • Guilds and chat

    Join a guild, fight together and talk with other players in a protected chat.

  • Gear and talents

    Crown, necklace, claws and armour across seven rarities to upgrade and merge, plus six talents to level up.

  • Seasonal events

    Halloween, the snow festival and more arrive through the year, each with its own shop and pass.

Free and fair

Everything can be earned by playing: dragons, costumes, gear, coins and gems. In-app purchases are optional, at fixed prices, and their content is always shown before you buy. There are no ads during a run. Bonus videos are always the player’s choice, never forced. The game never asks for your name, contacts or location; details are in the privacy policy (in French).

An age question is asked at the start, with settings suited to younger players: no free text in the chat under 13, only ready-made phrases.

Where and how to play

Dragonnet can be played in the browser of a phone, tablet or computer without installing anything: just open dragonnet.online/jouer. On Android, the app is available on Google Play. On iPhone, the game runs in the browser and can be added to the home screen. Your progress is saved, and a recovery code lets you restore it on another device.

Quick questions

Is Dragonnet really free?

Yes. Everything can be earned by playing. Purchases are optional, at fixed prices, and their content is always shown before you buy.

Do I need an account?

No. You may choose a nickname for the leaderboards and guilds, but the game never asks for your name, address or contacts.

How long does a run last?

Three minutes until the boss. It is short on purpose: a run fits in a queue or a break, and the rest of the game (the World, the garden, fishing) goes at your own pace.

Is the game suitable for children?

An age question is asked on the first launch and the settings adapt: under 13, the chat only offers ready-made phrases. Adults will find enough depth to stay interested, with talents, gear and duels.

Recent updates

Earlier versions brought guilds, the protected chat, leaderboards and the equal-arms ranked duel of Rush Dragon. The full timeline is on the version history page (in French).

In your language

The game automatically uses your phone or browser language, among more than forty languages including English, French, Spanish, Portuguese, German, Hindi, Japanese, Korean, Chinese and Arabic. You can change it at any time in the settings. The guides on this site are written in French by the game team.

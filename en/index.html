Dragonnet, the survival game with your baby dragon

Dragonnet is a free action game where you protect your nest’s treasure with a little dragon. Each run lasts three minutes, is played with one finger and ends with a boss fight. Play it in your browser, on iPhone, on PC and on Android.

Play now in your browser

No download and no account needed: the game starts right in the page. If you prefer an app, Dragonnet is also on Google Play. Both versions share the same game and the same servers.

How a run works

Your dragon attacks on its own. Your job is to move it: dodge the monsters, collect experience crystals and pick a new power at every level. After three minutes the chapter boss arrives. Win, and you bring coins, gems and sometimes new gear back to the nest.

  1. Keep moving. Monster waves grow every second. Standing still means getting surrounded.
  2. Pick your powers. Six weapons, six passives and their evolutions: no two runs play the same.
  3. Beat the boss. The witch, the dragon hunter and the black dragon each have attacks to learn.

What you will find in Dragonnet

  • Twelve legendary dragons

    From the Vouivre to Jörmungand, each dragon comes from a real legend and brings its own starting weapon and bonus.

  • Your dragon’s World

    Between runs, feed, train and care for your dragon, go fishing and grow magic rocks in your garden.

  • Rush Dragon

    A second game of duels: summon and merge your dragons, then face other players in arenas.

  • Guilds and chat

    Join a guild, fight together and talk with other players in a protected chat.

  • Gear and talents

    Crown, necklace, claws and armour across seven rarities to upgrade and merge, plus six talents to level up.

  • Seasonal events

    Halloween, the snow festival and more arrive through the year, each with its own shop and pass.

Free and fair

Everything can be earned by playing: dragons, costumes, gear, coins and gems. In-app purchases are optional, at fixed prices, and their content is always shown before you buy. There are no ads during a run. Bonus videos are always the player’s choice, never forced. The game never asks for your name, contacts or location; details are in the privacy policy (in French).

An age question is asked at the start, with settings suited to younger players: no free text in the chat under 13, only ready-made phrases.

In your language

The game automatically uses your phone or browser language, among more than forty languages including English, French, Spanish, Portuguese, German, Hindi, Japanese, Korean, Chinese and Arabic. You can change it at any time in the settings. The guides on this site are written in French by the game team.

Who makes Dragonnet
